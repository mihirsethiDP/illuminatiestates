This page explains how Illuminati Estates Pvt Ltd ("we", "us", "our") collects and uses information when you visit our website or submit an enquiry. It also contains important disclaimers regarding property listings, pricing and developer affiliations.

01

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal information you voluntarily provide through our website. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

By using this website and submitting enquiry or contact forms, you consent to the collection and use of information as described on this page.

02

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry or contact form, we may collect:

  • Your full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (if provided)
  • Message content and property of interest
  • The source page URL from which the enquiry was submitted

We may also collect basic technical information such as browser type and session data necessary for form security and rate limiting.

03

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our forms is used to:

  • Respond to your property enquiries and consultation requests
  • Contact you via phone, email, or WhatsApp regarding your interest
  • Provide relevant property information and advisory services
  • Maintain internal records of client communications

By submitting an enquiry or contact form, you consent to being contacted through the communication channels you provide, including phone calls, email, and WhatsApp messages related to your request.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We retain enquiry data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law.

04

Cookies & Analytics

This website may use session cookies necessary for form security and basic functionality. We may use third-party analytics tools in the future to understand website usage; such tools would be subject to their own privacy policies.

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.
