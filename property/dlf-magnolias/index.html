Overview

A super-luxury residential project by DLF, The Magnolias sits in Sector 42 along Golf Course Road, Gurgaon, and is ready for occupancy. This esteemed development spans 22 acres with five tall towers of 25 floors each, offering 4 to 6-bedroom configurations. The design focuses on space, comfort and luxurious living — each apartment offers a generous living area, well-proportioned bedrooms and expansive balconies with stunning views of the city skyline. The Magnolias enjoys proximity to excellent schools, top-notch healthcare and premier shopping, with superb connectivity to the rest of the city.
