Overview

A super-luxury residential project by DLF, promoted as a pinnacle of opulence among the residential complexes of northern India, Camellias represents the epitome of luxury. This multifaceted development presents a remarkable array of ultra-modern amenities and features, showcasing the utmost refinement and sophistication. Indulge your senses in a realm where leisure and convenience intertwine seamlessly, with a collection of unrivalled recreational facilities. Camellias is not just a residential complex; it is a lifestyle statement — a sanctuary where luxury takes on new meaning and surpasses even the loftiest of expectations.
