Overview

An ultra-premium 500 sq-yard corner kothi in DLF Phase 1, Gurugram — a rare residential offering for buyers who value privacy, space, elegance and address value. Spread across 500 sq yards, this premium 5-bedroom residence reflects refined living with a graceful architectural presence, warm interiors and well-planned indoor and outdoor spaces. Its corner positioning gives strong frontage, enhanced openness and improved natural light. The home features spacious living areas, elegant formal seating, a beautifully maintained private lawn and premium wooden finishes. Located in one of Gurugram’s most sought-after addresses, it enjoys excellent connectivity to Golf Course Road, MG Road, Cyber City, premium schools, luxury hotels and high-end retail.
