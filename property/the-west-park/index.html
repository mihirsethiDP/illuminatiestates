Overview

The West Park is a luxury residential development in Andheri, Mumbai, being developed in partnership with the Trident Group across an approximately 18-acre layout. The current phase brings eight towers over 5.18 acres, with a focus on premium 3 BHK residences — including 3 BHK with staff, and 3 BHK with staff and study — alongside select 4 BHK homes and exclusive penthouses.

Key Specifications

  • Possession: New launch
  • Total land area: 5.6 acres (part of an ~18-acre layout)
  • Initial launch: 4 of 8 towers
  • Separate entry & exit for residential and rehab zones
  • Direct access via the 90-ft-wide new JVLR Road
  • Secondary access from New Link Road
  • Western creek views & green Parsi Colony outlook
  • 4 Basement + Ground + Podium + Eco Deck + 35 floors
  • Initial offer: 416 units across 4 towers
  • All surface car parking
  • Dedicated drop-off lobbies for residents & visitors
  • Developed with the Trident Group (DLF 51% : Trident 49%)
