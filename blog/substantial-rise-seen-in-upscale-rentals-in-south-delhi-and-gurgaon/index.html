Substantial Rise Seen in Upscale Rentals in South Delhi & Gurgaon

Market analysts report a notable surge in high-end leasing within the prime regions of South Delhi and Gurgaon's Golf Course Road, attributed to the return of senior executives and expatriates to the workplace.

Rentals along Gurgaon's Golf Course Road have observed a 14% year-on-year increase. Similarly, residences within DLF5 Golf Links — including The Magnolias, The Aralias, The Camellias, and The Crest — have witnessed a twofold rise in rentals since 2020.

Sitting along Golf Course Road, the residences within DLF5 Golf Links hold sway over the luxury and ultra-luxury rental segments nationwide. The region has experienced a spike in rental demand from expatriates, business professionals, and CXOs residing in the Delhi-NCR, as well as prospective homeowners aiming to shift from bungalows to upscale condominiums.

The heightened desire for premium properties extends beyond the primary market; the secondary market is also grappling with a scarcity of such homes. This scarcity is particularly pronounced in coveted locations within major cities like Delhi, Mumbai, and Bengaluru.

In 2022, the mean monthly rent for a three-bedroom luxury residence in South Delhi climbed to ₹2.5 lakh — an escalation from ₹2.25 lakh in 2021 and ₹1.75 lakh in 2020.
